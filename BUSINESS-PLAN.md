# Overstood — Product Business Plan

## What is Overstood?

**Overstood** is a universal curiosity app. Type or snap anything — a fire hydrant, gravity, antibiotics, elevators — and get an instant, clear explanation.

The name "Overstood" captures that moment when something finally clicks. It's a real word (African American dialect), it's ownable, and it's memorable.

**Tagline:** *Get Overstood*

**URL:** overstood.app (pending)

---

## The Problem

People encounter things every day they don't understand:
- How does a refrigerator actually work?
- Why is the sky blue?
- What happens inside a barcode scanner?

Google exists but:
- Search results are cluttered and overwhelming
- Wikipedia is too dense
- YouTube requires watching 10 minutes
- Most explanations assume prior knowledge

People feel dumb but they shouldn't have to.

---

## The Solution

Overstood gives you a **instant clear explanation** in seconds.

- Type or snap a photo of anything
- Get a "smart friend" explanation — conversational, not academic
- Optionally engage with a quick curiosity quiz first
- Explore further with a "Try it" challenge

No accounts. No complexity. No judgment.

---

## The Product

### Core Flow

```
1. INPUT → Type or snap anything
2. QUIZ → Quick curiosity check (optional, 3 questions)
3. LESSON → Clear explanation with hook + what + how + why
4. CHALLENGE → One real-world action to reinforce learning
5. PROGRESS → Discovery count + streak tracking
```

### Features

- **Text input** — Type anything: "black holes", "voting", "insurance"
- **Photo input** — Snap a thing, get an explanation
- **Depth selector** — Quick / Standard / Deep explanations
- **Curiosity Quiz** — 3 questions before the lesson (prediction, experience, intuition)
- **Explanation format** — Hook, What is This, How it Works, Why it Matters, Key Ideas, Try It, Question
- **Share card** — Downloadable PNG to share what you learned
- **Discovery tracking** — Count of things explored
- **Streak tracking** — Consecutive days of exploration
- **Micro-achievements** — First discovery, 5 discoveries, first challenge, camera use

### Design

- Dark theme (#0D0D1A background)
- Orange (#FF6B35) + Gold (#FFD700) gradient accent
- Mobile-first, minimal UI
- Conversational AI voice — smart friend over coffee
- No academic tone, no jargon, no "Did you know?"

## Target Market

**Primary:** Curious people ages 13+ who want quick, clear explanations without friction.

**Secondary:**
- Parents answering kids' questions
- Students looking for quick clarity
- Lifelong learners who hate dense documentation
- Anyone who wants to feel less dumb about everyday things

**Not targeting:**
- Children under 13 (existing educational apps cover this)
- Academic or formal learning contexts
- Power users who want depth over speed

## Revenue Model

### Phase 1 (Free — Current)

Goal: Build users and validate product-market fit.

- Free for everyone
- API costs covered by founder (~$0.0002 per explanation)
- Gather feedback, iterate, prove retention

### Phase 2 (Freemium)

After validation (1000+ active users):

**Free tier:**
- 10 explanations per month
- Basic depth
- Core quiz and challenge features

**Premium tier ($4.99/month or $39.99/year):**
- Unlimited explanations
- All depth levels
- Save & revisit explanations
- Share library
- Early access to new features

### Phase 3 (Expansion)

- **Affiliate links** — Product recommendations within explanations (Amazon Associates)
- **School licensing** — Schools pay for student accounts ($2-5/student/year)
- **API access** — Developers pay to use the explanation engine
- **White-label** — Publishers embed Overstood in their platforms

## Risk Factors

1. **API costs scale with users** — Mitigate with freemium model and usage limits
2. **OpenAI dependency** — Mitigate by architecture that can swap to other models
3. **Low retention if explanation quality is poor** — Mitigate with continuous prompt refinement
4. **User acquisition** — Mitigate with organic sharing (shareable cards are the viral loop)
5. **Competition from Big Tech** — Mitigate by staying niche and frictionless

---

## Why This Will Work

1. **Frictionless** — Type or snap. Done.
2. **Shareable** — Each explanation is a shareable moment
3. **Addictive loop** — Curiosity → Learn → Discover → Repeat
4. **Universal** — Works for any human, any topic
5. **Ownable brand** — "Overstood" is distinctive and memorable

The product rewards curiosity. Every time someone uses it, they come back. That's the whole business.
