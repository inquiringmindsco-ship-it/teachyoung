# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 4, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Photos:** Images you upload are processed to generate explanations and are not stored on our servers beyond the session.
- **Text Input:** The topics you submit are used to generate explanations.
- **Usage Data:** Discovery counts, streaks, and achievements are stored locally on your device.

### Automatically Collected
- **No analytics trackers** (we don't use Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or similar)
- **No cookies** used for tracking
- **Basic server logs:** Hosting provider (Vercel) collects standard server logs including request timestamps and approximate location

## 2. How We Use Information

- **To provide the Service:** Process your inputs and generate explanations
- **To improve the Service:** We review usage patterns to understand what works
- **To communicate with you:** Only if you contact us first

## 3. AI Processing

- Your inputs are sent to OpenAI's API to generate explanations
- OpenAI processes this data under their [Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/privacy)
- We do not sell your inputs to third parties

## 4. Data Storage

- **Local Storage:** Usage data (discoveries, streaks) is stored in your browser's localStorage only
- **Server Storage:** We do not maintain user accounts or store your explanation history on our servers
- **Hosting Logs:** Vercel may retain server logs per their privacy policy

## 5. Third-Party Services

We use:
- **OpenAI** — For AI explanation generation (data processed per OpenAI's Privacy Policy)
- **Vercel** — For hosting and deployment (data processed per Vercel's Privacy Policy)

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access:** Ask what data we have (we can only tell you what we don't store — which is most of it)
- **Delete:** Clear your localStorage to remove your local usage data
- **Opt-out:** Simply don't use the Service if you disagree with these practices

## 8. Data Security

We use:
- HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
- No sensitive data stored on our servers
- Access limited to the minimum necessary for service operation

## 9. International Users

If you're outside the US, note that your data may be transferred to and processed in the US.

## 10. Changes to Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting.
